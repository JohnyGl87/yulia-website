The talk

From Hesitation to Action

The mental toolkit for decision-making and change, made with courage and vitality — five tools participants take with them out of the room.

The neurobiology of change

How the brain reads uncertainty, and how to turn a stress response into energy and focus.

Mental de-risking

A structured tool for dissolving catastrophizing and calming the stress system at the moment of decision.

The 70/30 decision rule

How to act effectively under uncertainty, without waiting for the 100% certainty that blocks execution.

Pace and discipline over emotion

A model borrowed from long-distance running — for consistency and focus under load and burnout.

PERMA for organizational flourishing

Strengthening employee engagement and the personal and professional sense of capability.

About

Yulia Glozman

Process engineer · Holistic coach · Speaker

I spent most of my professional life managing complex, strategic projects across several countries — a world where decisions are made under pressure, on tight timelines and without complete information. That is where I learned that the real obstacle is almost never technical knowledge, but what happens in our heads right before we commit.

Today I connect that hard engineering and management thinking with positive psychology and mental tools for personal and organizational growth. I don't ask people to "think positive" — I give them a method.

Process engineer — full engineering background
MBA — specializing in project management
Certified holistic coach — body, emotion and mind together
International experience — complex, strategic projects in several countries
