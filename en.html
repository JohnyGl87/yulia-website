About

Nice to meet you, I'm Yulia

By day, I'm an engineer and project manager. For years I have lived and breathed complex processes, goals, fine detail, problem-solving and pushing forward — to reach the most precise results.

But alongside the technological, engineering world, a powerful heart has always beaten in me. My greatest love is people. Out of curiosity and a deep passion for human nature and the human mind, I set out and studied the world of coaching and life-coaching.

Today I bring the best of both worlds into my coaching toolkit: professional knowledge, practical tools and the ability to break any challenge into steps — and together we bring it straight down to earth, into your real life.
